Rules

Do
  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter “o”.
  • Minimum size: 120 px wide on screens, 30 mm in print. Below that, use the icon only.
  • Use the white logo on dark backgrounds and photos.
Don't
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or glows to the logo.
  • Recolour it outside the palette below, or retype the wordmark in another font.
  • Put the full-colour logo on mid-tone or busy backgrounds.

The SVG files were traced from the website's 696 × 147 px logo and are sharp at any screen size. For large-format print (signage, vehicles), ask the original designer for the master vector file.

04 · Typography

Two typefaces

Headings · Manrope · 500–600 · tight letter-spacing (−2%)
Meet the agents that never clock out.
Body · Inter · 400–500 · 16–18 px on screens

Each agent learns your business — services, prices, policies and tone — and connects to your systems, so it gets things done instead of just chatting.

Both are free (Google Fonts / Fontsource). Small labels: Inter 600, uppercase, wide letter-spacing (+20%), in teal. Where the fonts aren't available (Word, email), use Arial.

05 · Voice

How we write

ClearShort sentences and everyday words. Say what the agent does: “books appointments”, not “leverages AI to optimise engagement”.
PracticalLead with the customer's problem (missed calls, messages at night) and the concrete result.
HonestNo invented numbers, clients or guarantees. Examples are labelled as examples. Say what an agent can't do.
