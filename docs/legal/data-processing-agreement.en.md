## 3. Processor obligations
The Processor shall:
- process the data only on the Controller's documented instructions (this agreement and the order);
- not use the data for its own purposes, sell it, or use it to train AI models;
- ensure that everyone with access is bound by confidentiality;
- apply appropriate technical and organisational security measures, including access control,
  encryption in transit, and logging of administrative access;
- help the Controller respond to requests from people exercising their rights (access, correction,
  deletion, objection);
- notify the Controller **without undue delay, and at the latest within 48 hours**, after becoming
  aware of a personal data breach, with the information available at the time;
- assist the Controller with data protection impact assessments and with the supervisory authority
  (the Information and Data Protection Commissioner) where needed;
- make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance with this agreement.

5.1 Conversation records are kept for **[90] days** so the Controller can review them and the agent
can be improved, then deleted, unless the Controller instructs otherwise in writing.

**[30] days**, unless the law requires it to be kept.

## 6. Controller obligations
The Controller confirms it has a lawful basis for the processing, informs its customers (for example
in its privacy policy and in the agent's first message) that they are talking to an AI agent, and
decides which tasks the agent may perform and which cases go to staff.
